# Mindseeker English glossary and voice guide

## Tone

A warm, natural late-1980s NES localization: plain words, short sentences, a little charm.
Never stiff, never machine-like, never modern slang. Mixed case with normal punctuation.

## Voices

| Speaker | Voice |
|---|---|
| Esper Kiyota (エスパーキヨタ, Masuaki Kiyota) | Warm, confident psychic mentor. Encouraging, a bit brotherly, sure of himself. Uses ぼく and ～だぞ / ～だね: "you can do it" energy, never cold or lecturing. Speaks of "vibrations" and visions sincerely. |
| The Center's computer (lines after 「) | Polite, clear, lightly formal system voice. Short instructions. |
| Narration (～ぞ！ observations such as なにも かんじないぞ！) | The player's own inner voice: short, punchy, present tense. |
| Shinobu (しのぶ) | Bubbly, flirty "gal" hostess. Exclamation marks, playful. Keep it cute and PG. |
| Guru (グルー), a Psi Master | Stern, testing, dignified. |
| Jericho (ジエリコ) | Courteous, formal. |
| Mami (マミ) | Friendly, casual young woman. |
| Psychic City residents | Casual and varied; each a quick sketch of a personality. |

Politeness shifts matter: the computer and instructors use です/ます (polite), Kiyota uses plain
form (familiar). Keep that contrast in English through word choice, not through stiffness.

Kiyota addresses the player by name with no honorific once training starts; early lines use
～さん. Render ～さん as the bare name (no "Mr./Ms.", no "-san").

## Script tokens

- `{NAME}` the player's name (up to 4 letters).
- `{V}` one character filled in by the game at run time; a run of `{V}` is one field
  (a number or a short word). Keep every run intact and in order.
